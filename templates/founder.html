Meet the founder

Black Coffee was started by a Colombian coffee lover who wanted to bring the country's best-known brands and small organic growers straight to people abroad, family, friends and anyone who misses a real Colombian cup.

Every coffee in this shop is one I would serve my own family — sourced from growers and roasters I trust, and shipped the way I'd want it to arrive at my own door.
